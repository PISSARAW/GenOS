# GENOS Philosophical Constitution

*GenOS's foundational philosophical commitments. These principles are not metaphors — they are
operational constraints that govern how the system treats knowledge, identity, causality, and change.*

---

## I. Processual Ontology

An agent is not a substance with properties; it is a **versioned process** maintaining continuity through transformation.

- **Primitive**: `Agent = lineage + genome + history + current phenotype + commitments + causal relations + environment`
- **Snapshot**: Not "the agent"; but a **temporal state** of the agentic process
- **Fork/Merge**: Agents can be forked, mutated, replicated, stopped, revived, recombined
- **Implication**: Identity must be analyzed across multiple dimensions, not assumed

---

## II. Fallibilist Epistemology

Success does not equal truth; a claim is not evidence; knowledge is revisable.

- `success != truth`
- `claim != evidence`
- Provenance ≠ truth of payload
- Consensus ≠ truth
- Replay ≠ necessary reproduction of the external world
- **Any proof from the same LLM is not independent evidence**

---

## III. Causal Interventionism

Causality is tested by intervention, not assumed from correlation or outcome comparison.

- **Do-calculus approach**: `world W` → `do(remove X)` → compare `Y₀` vs `Y₁`
- **Fork + replay + intervention** is the primitive, not counterfactual reasoning alone
- `isDeterministic()` must trace causal paths, not just compare final outcomes
- **Regularity checks** must verify cause→effect relationships with controlled variables
- Causal conclusions require **causal receipts** documenting interventions and results

---

## IV. Identity by Continuity and Lineage

An agent can be qualitatively identical yet numerically distinct across fork/merge boundaries.

- **Token identity**: Numerical identity across time (same agent ID over time)
- **Genomic identity**: Shared genome/lineage heritage
- **Lineage identity**: Descent relationship (parent → child → descendant)
- **Phenotypic identity**: Current capabilities and state
- **Mission identity**: Shared purpose or task context
- **Memory continuity**: Continuity of experiential/trace history
- **Causal continuity**: Unbroken chain of causal influences
- **Functional identity**: Same role/function within the system
- **Genos can answer**: "These two agents are identical per X, but distinct per Y"

---

## V. Evidence Algebra (Typed, Not Scored)

Evidence is not homogenized into a single score; different types carry different properties and independence guarantees.

| Type | Properties |
|------|-----------|
| Observational | source_reliability, recency, scope |
| Experimental | reproducibility, causal_relevance, coverage |
| Formal | proof, falsifiability, mathematical certainty |
| Causal | intervention-documented, causal_graph, SCM |
| Testimonial | author_authority, independence, conflict of interest |
| Replicated | independent_replication, same_result_consistency |
| Adversarial | challenge success rate, falsification record |
| Human-authoritative | domain_expertise, peer_verification, track_record |

- **Two proofs from the same LLM are not independent**
- Evidence types have **dependencies** and **independence** relations
- `evidenceScore` is replaced by evidence **profiles** with explicit limitations

---

## VI. Biomimetic Experimentalism

Nature is a collection of search processes, not a database of solutions, nor an objective function.

- **Maxim**: `Nature is not an objective function`
- **Chain**: `natural phenomenon → extract invariant → computational hypothesis → implementation → ablation → measurable benefit? → retain/reject`
- **No biological analogy grants runtime authority**
- Evolution can produce extinction, waste, local optima, historical lock-in
- **Observed in nature ≠ desirable in GenOS**
- Only invariants that are **computationally measurable and empirically useful** are retained

---

## VII. Pluralist Modalities

Multiple possible worlds, branches, and coexisting frameworks are the default, not the exception.

- **Forks, worlds, and branches** are first-class citizen concepts
- No single "timeline" is privileged; all experimental branches are valid subjects
- `possible-worlds` and `contingency-necessity` are operational modalities, not metaphysical claims
- **Modal plurality** is the default state of the system

---

## VIII. Authority of the Human, Not Philosophy

No philosophical consensus, authority, or tradition transforms a belief into truth within the runtime.

- **No consensus → truth**
- **No success → proof**
- **No adaptation → improvement** without external criteria
- Philosophical traditions serve as **competing interpretive lenses**, not governing authorities
- The only runtime authority comes from **evidence gates**, **leases**, and **circuit breakers**

---

## IX. Revisability

All knowledge, structures, and configurations are subject to revision when confronted with new evidence or changing conditions.

- No concept is frozen at `implemented` status permanently
- Services marked `executable: false` are explicit about their non-authoritative status
- **If a theory cannot be refuted by the data provided to it, it remains a lens, not a truth claim**
- The system must have formal mechanisms for **retirement** and **deprecation** of concepts

---

## X. Experimental Philosophy over Simulated Philosophy

Philosophical frameworks in GenOS are tools for critique and analysis, not simulations of historical philosophers.

- **Do not ask**: "Is the agent stoic?"
- **Do ask**: "What would a stoic reading reveal about this decision?"
- Frameworks produce: `assessment, assumptions, questions, tradeoffs, unresolved_conflicts, missing_information`
- Multiple analyses are juxtaposed without claiming any philosophy has "decided"
- **No verdict** is emitted; only assessments with explicit assumptions and limits

---

## Constitution Status

This document replaces all previous implicit philosophical assumptions. It is a **living constitution** — subject to
the same revisability principle (Principle IX) that it codifies.
