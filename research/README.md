# Research archive

This directory preserves exploratory work that informed GenOS without mixing
it with the maintained product entry points.

| Area | Contents | Support status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`reverse-game-of-life/`](reverse-game-of-life/) | Solvers, datasets, Rust prototypes, and historical outputs | Research archive |
| [`tsp/`](tsp/) | Travelling-salesperson evaluators and candidate solvers | Research archive |
| [`swarm/`](swarm/) | Historical swarm-memory artifact | Research archive |

Files here are retained for provenance. They are not part of the stable GenOS
API, and a passing product CI run does not imply that every exploratory solver
has been validated. Each area documents its own working directory and inputs.
